# Hiring in a time of AI

For those in the same position as me, you will probably recognize the pattern:

Create a hiring notice -> getting flodded by applicants -> filter for the qualified applicants -> send out a small coding tests -> filtering for qualified applicants -> interview with a leetcode test -> potential hire.

This process often takes a lot of time, and you sometimes accidentally filter out good candidates by accident as most applicants are using AI.
